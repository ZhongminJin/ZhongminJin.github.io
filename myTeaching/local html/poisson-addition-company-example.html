Adding Independent Poisson Variables

Example: a company receives complaints through two independent channels.

Phone complaints arrive at an average rate of 2 per hour, so X ~ Poisson(2).

Website complaints arrive at an average rate of 5 per hour, so Y ~ Poisson(5).

Therefore, X + Y ~ Poisson(2 + 5) = Poisson(7).
