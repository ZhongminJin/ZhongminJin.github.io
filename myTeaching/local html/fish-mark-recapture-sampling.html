Fish Pond Sampling Distributions

One pond, three sampling questions: estimate the total number of fish, estimate the average fish weight, then combine them to estimate the total fish biomass. Each random sample gives a statistic; repeated samples show the sampling distribution of that statistic.

1. Mark-recapture: estimate how many fish are in the lake

Classroom story

  1. Catch M fish, mark them, then release them.
  2. Later catch n fish. Count how many of these are already marked: R.
  3. If the marked fish mixed randomly, R/n should be close to M/N.
  4. So N can be estimated by N̂ = Mn/R.

5. Conditions to say out loud

Closed population: no births, deaths, immigration, or emigration during sampling.
Marked fish mix randomly back into the lake.
Tags stay on and are always spotted.
Every fish has roughly the same chance of being caught or weighed.
