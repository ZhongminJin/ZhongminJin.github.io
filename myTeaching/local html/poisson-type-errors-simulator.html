Has the average event rate changed?

An exact Poisson hypothesis test where X counts events during a fixed exposure.

Choose an average-rate question

Help-desk calls

A help desk records incoming telephone calls. We count how many calls arrive during a fixed observation period.

Why Poisson? Calls are treated as independent arrivals occurring singly at a roughly constant average rate.

One eventOne incoming call
Exposure unitOne hour
Question under testIs λ greater than 3 calls per hour?

Experiment controls

H₀: λ = 3.00
H₁: λ > 3.00
Under H₀: X ~ Po(12.00)
The Poisson model assumes events occur independently, singly, and at a constant average rate within the observation period.
Repeated-test results will appear in the final panel.

Why is X Poisson?

X = number of events in exposure T
  • Events occur independently
  • Events occur singly
  • The average rate λ is constant
  • The expected count is Tλ
Under H₀: X ~ Po(Tλ₀)
