Interpretation

Two-tailed convention used here

The calculator assigns α/2 to each tail when finding the two-tailed critical region. The reported two-tailed p-value is twice the smaller one-tailed probability, capped at 1. Some courses or software may use a different exact two-sided convention.

Why can α* be smaller than α?

Binomial and Poisson statistics take integer values. Moving a cut-off by one whole count can make the rejection probability jump across α, so the valid critical region generally has probability below the chosen significance level.
